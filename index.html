The Problem with Formal Financial Statements

Excel Templates for Preparing Financial Statement Presentations

  • The number one choice for accountants
  • Reliant on system reports that rely on the COA
  • A multi-step manual process
  • Impractical for any other purpose
  • Single use — every period is a repeat process

The problem isn't effort — it's that the template's usefulness depends entirely on one company's Chart of Accounts. A new client means rebuilding from scratch. A changed account means patching whatever just broke.

For accountants who build these templates by hand, that's a rebuild-and-patch cycle eating time that should go to analysis. For bookkeepers who hand over the system's own reports instead, the result rarely looks as polished as what a CPA firm would deliver — even when the underlying work is just as rigorous.

The bp-Reports Solution

One Framework, Every Client

  • Independent of any single company's Chart of Accounts
  • Fully automated — no manual rebuild each period
  • The same structure works for every client, every time
  • Professionally formatted, presentation-ready output
  • Visual KPI dashboards included

Because the structure doesn't depend on any one client's Chart of Accounts, there's nothing to rebuild when you take on a new client, and nothing to patch when an account changes.

The same framework that works for your largest client works for your smallest — accountants get their time back, and bookkeepers get output that looks like it came from a CFO's office.
