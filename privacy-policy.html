By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, do not access or use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, company name, and billing details provided when you register or manage your account

  • Usage information — information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and features used, collected automatically to help us maintain and improve the Service
  • Payment information — processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not directly collect or store your full payment card details

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including generating the reports you request
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Communicate with you about your account, including service-related notices and support
  • Monitor and improve the security, performance, and reliability of the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Your Data

You retain ownership of Your Data. We access and process Your Data solely as necessary to provide the Service to you — for example, to calculate and generate the financial reports you request. We do not sell Your Data, and we do not use Your Data to train products or services for other customers.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — with third parties who perform services on our behalf, including payment processing (Stripe) and cloud hosting and data storage (Microsoft Azure), solely to the extent necessary for them to provide those services to us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain account information and Your Data for as long as your account remains active. We may retain certain information longer where required for legal, accounting, or legitimate business purposes.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. You may update your account information directly within the Service, or contact us using the information below to make a request.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies necessary for the Service to function properly, such as keeping you signed in. We do not use these technologies to track you across third-party websites for advertising purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Users

The Service is hosted in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.
